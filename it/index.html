KPI strip e telemetria di portafoglio

Il KPI strip trasforma l’attività grezza dei deal in segnali istituzionali utili ai comitati di allocazione. Invece di metriche isolate, Fuelaris unisce profondità pipeline, copertura governance, velocità di execution e prontezza di liquidità in un unico livello operativo. Portfolio model

Our model combines core, opportunistic, and reserve sleeves so each mandate keeps strategic exposure while retaining tactical flexibility. Core positions target durable IT operators with proven governance and reporting hygiene. Opportunistic positions focus on temporary dislocations where underwriting confidence exceeds market consensus. A dedicated liquidity sleeve protects execution quality during uncertain conditions.

Process timeline

Fuelaris Capital follows a five-gate process: sourcing, diligence, investment committee, execution, and continuous monitoring. Each gate contains clear documentation standards and compliance checks, reducing decision noise and improving accountability. For investors, this means transparent progression and faster re-allocation when conditions change.
